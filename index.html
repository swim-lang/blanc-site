Introduction

A strong point of view. Plenty of room for yours.

Blanc isn’t an empty box, and it won’t decide the day for you. Old brick, whitewashed walls, open sky, and three distinct spaces give you a beautiful beginning. From there, the day becomes entirely your own.

Creative Freedom

Not a package. A point of departure.

There is no single way Blanc is supposed to look. We’ll provide the framework, practical support, and a place with enough character to inspire you without taking over.

Our Story

We started here, too.

Before Blanc became ours to care for, it was where we got married.

We know the strange, wonderful feeling of walking into these rooms and imagining everyone you love inside them. We know how a place becomes inseparable from what happened there.

That experience still shapes how we think about Blanc. A venue should offer more than a beautiful backdrop. It should inspire you, support you, and hold the day without taking it over.

Now we get to help other people make the place their own. We don’t take that lightly.

How many guests can Blanc host?

Blanc is designed for celebrations of up to 200 guests, with layouts that can feel just as considered at a smaller scale. Tell us your approximate guest count and we’ll help you think through the best flow.

N.01
Is Blanc both indoors and outdoors?

Yes. The Gallery, Barn, and Courtyard give your event room to move between different settings and atmospheres throughout the day.

N.02
How much can we change?

Quite a lot. The space is meant to be interpreted. We’ll share practical guidelines and requirements early, then work with you and your creative partners to make the venue feel personal.

N.03
What comes with the venue?

Your proposal will clearly outline access, on-site support, available furnishings, audiovisual equipment, décor, and vendor requirements. The practical details should feel just as considered as the visible ones.

N.04
Can Blanc host something other than a wedding?

Absolutely. Private dinners, milestone celebrations, showers, company gatherings, nonprofit events, creative productions, and other occasions that deserve a memorable setting.

N.05
Can we come see it?

Please do. Photographs show the texture; a visit reveals how naturally the spaces connect.
